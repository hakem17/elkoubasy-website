Frozen Fruits

Our IQF fruits are rapidly frozen at controlled temperatures to help preserve their natural flavor, color, texture, and quality. Individual pieces remain separate for easy handling, portioning, and processing, providing manufacturers with a reliable fruit ingredient for year-round production.

What do you need to know about our frozen fruits?

Straight answers for buyers sourcing frozen fruit at industrial scale.

Which cuts and formats are available?

Strawberry (whole, halves, slices and diced), mango (chunks, slices and diced) and pomegranate arils. Strawberry and pomegranate come in bulk: big bags and 10 kg cartons. Mango also comes in 1 kg, 2.5 kg and 10 kg packs.

What are your frozen fruits used for?

Dairy. Fruit yogurts, smoothies, milk drinks, ice cream and frozen desserts.

Beverage. Smoothies, fruit blends and juice preparations, adding fruit content, flavor and color.

Bakery and confectionery. Fillings, pastries, cakes and baked inclusions, plus chocolates, gummies and toppings.

Can you match our specification?

Yes. We cut to the size you ask for, and packaging can be customised. We also have fixed standard cut sizes for mango and strawberry; ask us for the sizes.

What is the minimum order?

One full container. It can hold one product or a mix of products.

How long from order to dispatch?

3–4 weeks after the order is confirmed and the advance payment is made.

How long does it keep?

24 months at −18 °C. Store it at −18 °C or colder.

Can we get a sample or a spec sheet first?

Yes. Standard samples (2.5 kg) are free; you pay shipping and customs. Custom samples may be charged. Choose “Request a sample” or “Request a spec sheet” in the Contact form.

Where do you export, and on which terms?

We export to the UAE, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Iraq, Canada and the Netherlands, and North America is one of our strongest markets. Ex Works (EXW) is our standard term; CIF, FOB or CFR on request. We ship from Suez, Port Said, Sokhna, Alexandria and Safaga.
