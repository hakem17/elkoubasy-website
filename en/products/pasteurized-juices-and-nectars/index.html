Pasteurized Juices & Nectars

We sell pasteurized drinks under two brands. Joyce is 100% fruit juice with no added sugar. Aloha is a fruit nectar made with 30% fruit.

Both are made from carefully selected fruit and pasteurized under controlled conditions, which protects their safety, flavor and color and gives them a dependable shelf life. Each comes in 250 ml and 1 L bottles.

What do you need to know about our pasteurized juices and nectars?

Straight answers on pasteurized juice packaging, export, and samples for industrial buyers.

What is the difference between Joyce and Aloha?

Joyce is 100% fruit juice with no added sugar. Aloha is a fruit nectar: 30% fruit, with water added.

What are your pasteurized juices used for?

Beverage. Our pasteurized juices are integrated into ready-to-drink juices, nectars, fruit beverages, blends, and juice-based drinks, providing consistent fruit flavor and quality for commercial beverage production.

Dairy. Our pasteurized juices are incorporated into fruit yogurts, cultured dairy drinks, milk-based fruit beverages, and dairy desserts, adding fruit flavor, color, and acidity to the formulation.

What packaging formats are available?

We fill our pasteurized juices under controlled hygienic conditions into suitable food-grade packaging, including 250 ml and 1 L bottles [TBC: glass, as in the pack renders?], designed to preserve product quality and freshness while providing a convenient and secure packaging solution.

Do you export internationally?

Yes. We ship to Europe, Canada, and the Middle East, including the GCC and Iraq. Our export team can confirm transit times and documentation for your destination.

Can I get samples?

Yes. Small standard samples are generally free, subject to availability. The customer covers courier, customs, and temperature-controlled shipping costs. Customized samples or trial runs may be chargeable.

Do you supply pasteurized juice to manufacturers?

Yes. Besides the Joyce and Aloha bottles, we supply juice in aseptic packaging for food and beverage manufacturers.

What is the minimum order?

One full container. It can hold one product or a mix of products.
