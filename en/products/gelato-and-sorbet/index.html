What do you need to know about our gelato and sorbet?

Answers on fruit character, texture, and the work behind sorbets and gelatos.

Which fruits work best for your gelato and sorbet lines?

Mango, strawberry, lemon, guava, and pomegranate are particularly suitable. Other fruits and blends can be developed subject to availability and testing.

Where are your gelato and sorbet used?

Dairy. Our gelato is used in dairy applications to create rich, creamy frozen desserts with a variety of flavors. Sorbet provides a fruit-based, dairy-free alternative.

Bakery and confectionery. Gelato and sorbet complement bakery products as fillings, toppings and dessert accompaniments, adding flavor, texture and a premium finish to cakes and pastries. In confectionery, they can be incorporated into frozen treats, layered desserts and chocolate-based products, offering versatile flavor combinations and a refreshing fruit or creamy profile.

Can sweetness levels be adjusted for each customer or market?

Yes. Sweetness, fruit content, acidity, texture, and flavor intensity can be adjusted according to customer preferences and market requirements.

How do you prevent iciness and crystallization?

We use controlled formulation, proper sugar balancing, stabilizers where required, rapid freezing, controlled mixing, and continuous cold-chain management.

What packaging formats are available?

Available formats include single-serving cups, retail tubs, and larger food-service tubs. Pack sizes, labels, and private-label designs can be customized.

Are frozen desserts sold to businesses or consumers?

Our gelato and sorbet products are supplied internationally to business partners as manufacturing and private-label solutions, and our branded finished products also reach end consumers in different markets.

Which flavours do you make?

Five gelato flavours (hazelnut, wild berries yogurt, chocolate, vanilla and mastic) and three fruit sorbets (strawberry, lemon and mango).

What is the minimum order?

One full container. It can hold one product or a mix of products.
