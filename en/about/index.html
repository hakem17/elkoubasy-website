Fruit & Juice Processing for 40 Years

At El Koubasy, decades of experience have refined the art and precision of fruit and juice processing.

Forty years of fruit, made for industry

El Koubasy began 40 years ago as a fresh juice and ice cream business in Downtown Cairo.

Today we manufacture and export fruit pulps, juice concentrates, juices, frozen fruit and vegetables and frozen desserts for food and beverage manufacturers, distributors and foodservice businesses in Egypt and abroad. A new mega factory and IQF complex in Giza is planned toward 2030.

Mission and Vision

We supply bulk ingredients and foodservice products to local and international markets, combining consistent quality with dependable supply and modern processing expertise.

01Our mission

To deliver consistent, high-quality food and beverage ingredients through disciplined processing, rigorous food safety standards and continuous innovation, creating lasting value for our customers and partners.

02Our vision

To be a leading global food and beverage manufacturer, distinguished by processing expertise, consistent quality, continuous progress and customer satisfaction.
