Who We Are?

Founded in Egypt 40 years ago, El Koubasy has grown from a local fresh juice company into a diversified fruit export company and industrial juice processor serving clients across international markets.

Today, we stand as one of the leading fruit juice manufacturers and fresh produce exporters in the region.

Our Production Capabilities

Modern processing and controlled production systems deliver consistent fruit and vegetable ingredients for every application.

  • Individual quick freezing

  • Aseptic processing and packaging

Sustainability & Responsible Growth

Responsible sourcing, efficient production and careful logistics, from our farms to your production line.

Responsible farming

We work with our growers to protect the land: crop rotation keeps the soil rich, and natural pest control protects nearby ecosystems.

Efficient production

Peels and pulp are upcycled, water is reused, and efficient machinery and LED lighting cut energy use.

Packaging and logistics

Recyclable packaging, precise pallet wrapping and well-planned loading mean less material and less energy in transport.
