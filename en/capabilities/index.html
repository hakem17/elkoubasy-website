Advanced Technology & Processing Solutions

Three production facilities, 24/7 operations and an end-to-end supply chain, all managed in-house.

Our Capabilities

600 professionals run three production facilities around the clock. Every stage, from raw material to export, is managed in-house: production, cold storage, the supply chain, export and private label.

Production & Technology

Our headquarters and main production are in the Third Industrial Zone of 6th of October City. Three facilities run 24/7 for high-volume orders.

  • FMC extractors

    High-efficiency extraction that recovers more juice from every ton of fruit.

  • Tower evaporators

    Low-energy concentration that keeps natural flavour and colour.

  • Aseptic lines

    Bag-in-drum filling for long shelf life without refrigeration.

  • IQF tunnels

    Quick freezing that keeps texture, colour and freshness.

Cold Storage

Raw materials and finished products are held between −30 °C and −18 °C, in our own cold stores and with trusted partners, from intake until they are ready to export. Every stage is documented.

Supply Chain Journey

One integrated process, from harvest to delivery.

  1. 1

    Receive and grade

    We source from established farms and trusted suppliers. Every intake is inspected, graded and checked in our in-house labs on arrival, so only approved material enters production.

  2. 2

    Cold store and process

    Raw materials are held in our cold stores at a controlled temperature, between −30 °C and −18 °C, then sorted, cut and prepared on our production lines.

  3. 3

    Shock freeze and load

    Where required, products are shock frozen with IQF, so each piece freezes separately. They are then packed to your specification and loaded with the export documents.

  4. 4

    Cold transport and delivery

    Temperature loggers travel with every shipment by road, sea or air, and each one is tracked until it reaches you in the required condition.

Export & Shipping

32% of our production is exported. We ship from Suez, Port Said, Sokhna, Alexandria and Safaga to Canada, the United States, Europe, the Middle East and the GCC.

Terms and minimum order

Ex Works as standard; CIF, FOB or CFR on request. One full container, single or mixed products.

Packaging

Aseptic drums, bulk totes, IQF master cartons and industrial bags, palletised for transit.

Documentation

Certificates of origin, health certificates and invoices are prepared in-house before loading.

Cold chain

Data loggers record the temperature from our facilities to the final destination.

What do you need to know about working with us?

Answers to common questions about lead times, orders and documentation.

What are your lead times?

The typical lead time is 3–4 weeks after order confirmation and advance payment. It may vary depending on the product, packaging, testing, raw-material availability and customization.

What is the minimum order?

For export orders, the typical minimum order is one full container (one product or mixed products). It may vary depending on the product, packaging format and minimum production batch.

How does custom formulation work?

We start from your brief and a technical review, run formulation trials and internal tests, and send you a sample. After your feedback we adjust it and move to final approval, with a production trial first if needed.

Do you offer product-development support and trial runs before a full order?

Yes. We provide formulation support, samples and trial production, subject to technical feasibility and agreed costs.

Do you handle export documentation?

Yes. Certificates of origin, health certificates and commercial invoices are prepared in-house. The paperwork is ready before the container leaves our facility.
